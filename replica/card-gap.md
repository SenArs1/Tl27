# Booking card: competitor vs Stayra (2026-10-23)

Sources: the public knowledge base of the original (booking editing, change history,
tags, moving a payment, short booking info) plus the earlier chessboard articles.
Summary in our own words.

| Feature | Competitor | Stayra (v6) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Card with tabs | Details · Accommodation and services · Invoices and payments · Documents · Notes · Change history | Детали · Проживание и услуги · Счёт и платежи · Гости и МВД · Документы · Заметки · История |
| Header with quick actions | panel of icons | status, tags, dates, rooms, amount/owed + check-in / no-show / cancel |
| Replace guest, customer | yes | guest's name and contacts; customer (company, phone, INN) — goes into the invoice |
| Purpose of trip, arrival and departure times | yes | yes |
| Tags from a reference list, mark on the chessboard, filter | yes | yes: tags in settings with colours, corner on the bar, filter on the chessboard and in the journal |
| Change accommodation price | yes | yes, with a required reason; tax and total recalculated |
| Discount | yes | % or ₽ on accommodation, with a reason |
| Add / change service | yes | from the services list (by its rule: per stay/night/guest) or a one-off line; removal |
| Several invoices for different payers | yes | ❌ one invoice per booking; customer appears in the invoice |
| Payment, cancelling a payment, refund | yes | payment; voiding (struck through, reason, not counted); refund not exceeding what was paid |
| Moving a payment to another booking | yes (via refund + manual payment) | in one action: amount and booking number, both bookings' history updated |
| Deposit | yes | ❌ not done |
| Documents: view, print | yes | booking confirmation, invoice, payment statement, guest forms |
| Notes and tasks with a deadline | yes | yes; tasks for today/overdue — on the dashboard and in the chessboard strip, open ones — in the side panel |
| Change history: when, who, what, was/became | yes | yes: every action, including creation (guest/channel/staff member) |
| Journal export to Excel | yes | CSV with the current filters, opens in Excel |
| Group bookings | yes | ❌ separate project |
